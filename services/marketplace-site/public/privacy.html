Privacy notice
1 August 2026

Plain-language privacy.

This notice covers Modeframe's public information site, channel-specific purchase verification, and buyer support. Shopify and each selling marketplace process information under their own notices.

Public site.

This site does not set analytics or advertising cookies and does not request account details. Infrastructure providers may process ordinary request metadata such as IP address, user agent, requested path, timestamp, and security logs to deliver and protect the service.

Purchase verification.

Creative Market purchases currently use the purchase information made available to the shop owner by Creative Market and do not require an external Modeframe activation code. If another marketplace supplies a purchase code, its channel-specific activation service may receive that code and the permanent Shopify myshopify.com domain. Supported purchase codes are verified with the selling marketplace and stored only as keyed cryptographic fingerprints, not as plaintext.

The purpose is to confirm a purchase, administer support and updates, prevent code sharing, and operate transfers, refunds, and revocations. Purchase verification never controls whether an installed theme renders.

Support.

Support processes the contact details and technical information a buyer supplies to answer the request, reproduce defects, prevent abuse, and maintain an audit trail. Buyers should remove customer, payment, order, receipt, and credential data before sending evidence.

Retention and sharing.

Purchase and support records are retained while needed to administer licences, support, updates, fraud prevention, disputes, and legal obligations. Expired rate-limit records are routinely removed. Information is shared only with service providers needed to operate Modeframe, with the selling marketplace where purchase verification requires it, when required by law, or with the buyer's direction.

Your request.

Use the support contact to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, or another applicable privacy right. Identity and purchase ownership may need to be verified before account-specific information is disclosed or changed.
