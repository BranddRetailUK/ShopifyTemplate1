Privacy notice
1 August 2026

Plain-language privacy.

This notice covers Modeframe’s public information site, purchase activation, and buyer support. Shopify and Envato process information under their own notices.

Public site.

This site does not set analytics or advertising cookies and does not request account details. Infrastructure providers may process ordinary request metadata such as IP address, user agent, requested path, timestamp, and security logs to deliver and protect the service.

Purchase activation.

The activation service receives an Envato purchase code and permanent Shopify myshopify.com domain. The purchase code is verified with Envato and stored only as a keyed cryptographic fingerprint, not as plaintext. The service stores the Envato item identifier, licensed store domain, theme version, license name, sale/support dates, activation status, and verification timestamps. Rate limiting uses short-lived keyed fingerprints of the purchase code and requesting address.

The purpose is to verify a purchase, bind support/update eligibility to one store, prevent code sharing, and operate transfers, refunds, and revocations. Activation never controls whether the installed theme renders.

Support.

Support processes the contact details and technical information a buyer supplies to answer the request, reproduce defects, prevent abuse, and maintain an audit trail. Buyers should remove customer, payment, order, and credential data before sending evidence.

Retention and sharing.

Activation records are retained while needed to administer the license, support, updates, fraud prevention, disputes, and legal obligations. Expired rate-limit records are routinely removed. Information is shared only with service providers needed to run the service, with Envato for purchase verification, when required by law, or with the buyer’s direction.

Your request.

Use the support contact to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, or another applicable privacy right. Identity and purchase ownership may need to be verified before account-specific information is disclosed or changed.
