Refund process

Refunds follow Envato’s rules.

Modeframe purchases made through ThemeForest are governed by the Envato Market Refund Rules and handled in the Envato refund workflow.

Before requesting a refund.

Check the documentation and open a support request for a reproducible theme issue. Include the Modeframe version, relevant URL, browser/device, expected result, actual result, and steps to reproduce. Do not send the purchase code in a public comment.

Submit through Envato.

Use the refund request link in the buyer’s Envato account. Envato notifies the author and provides an on-platform discussion area. Refund discussions should stay there so Envato can review the record if a dispute is raised. The author aims to respond within Envato’s five-day response window.

After an approved refund.

Envato processes the refund and revokes the buyer’s rights to use, download, and rate the item. Modeframe’s corresponding activation, support, and update eligibility may be revoked or retained only as necessary for dispute, fraud-prevention, or legal records.

This page summarizes the operating process and does not replace Envato’s current Refund Rules or rights that cannot legally be excluded.
