Refund process

Refunds follow the selling marketplace.

A Modeframe refund request is governed and processed by the marketplace where the purchase was completed.

Before requesting a refund.

Check the documentation and open a support request for a reproducible theme issue. Include the Modeframe version, relevant URL, browser/device, expected result, actual result, and steps to reproduce. Do not send purchase codes or receipts in a public comment.

Use the marketplace workflow.

Open the purchase in the buyer account and use that marketplace's support or refund workflow. Keep the discussion on-platform when the marketplace provides a case thread so it can review the record if needed.

After an approved refund.

The marketplace controls the refund and the buyer's continuing download and licence rights. Corresponding Modeframe support and update eligibility may be revoked or retained only as necessary for disputes, fraud prevention, or legal records.

This page summarizes the operating process and does not replace the marketplace's current refund rules or rights that cannot legally be excluded.
