Modeframe support.

Support availability follows the marketplace terms and licence selected when Modeframe was purchased.

Open a support request

Included.

  • Answers about built-in theme features and documented settings.
  • Help installing the supplied inner theme archive and following the update procedure.
  • Investigation and fixes for reproducible defects in an unmodified supported release.
  • Assistance with third-party assets included in the buyer download, if any.
  • Accessibility regressions caused by the unmodified theme.

Not included.

  • Store setup, catalog entry, migration, photography, copywriting, or bespoke design.
  • Custom code, new features, or substantial layout changes.
  • Debugging merchant/agency modifications or unrelated third-party app defects.
  • Shopify platform incidents, app configuration, checkout extensions, or external APIs.
  • Recovery of deleted themes, settings, orders, products, or customer data.

Send a useful report.

Include the permanent myshopify.com domain, Modeframe version, affected URL, browser/device, reproducible steps, expected result, actual result, and a screenshot or recording where useful. The initial-response objective is two UK business days.

Do not send passwords, API credentials, purchase codes, full receipts, payment details, or customer data in a public comment or initial request. If access is needed, support will request time-limited Shopify collaborator access with the minimum permissions required.
