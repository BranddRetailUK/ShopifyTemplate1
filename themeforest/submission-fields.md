purchase-options app but no particular app is bundled.

## Tags

Theme/Code items allow no more than 15 comma-separated lowercase tags. Use:

`editorial, fashion, lifestyle, beauty, accessories, minimalist, responsive, product filtering, predictive search, quick add, cart drawer, localization, selling plans, product video, online store 2.0`

## Short pitch

An editorial Shopify theme with six global styles, flexible product
storytelling, and native commerce features for visually led brands.

## Feature list

- Six storefront-wide visual styles in one install preset
- Editorial campaign, collection, product, journal, and story layouts
- Product variants, native swatches, rich media, unit pricing, pickup, selling
  plans, accelerated checkout, and gift-card recipients
- Predictive search, filters, sorting, recommendations, quick add, and drawer
  or page cart
- Flexible sticky header, nested navigation, localization, customer accounts,
  social links, payment icons, and Follow on Shop
- Hero, Slideshow, Scroll Bridge, Motion Accents, media, feature, testimonial,
  logo, blog, video, FAQ, newsletter, CTA, and Custom Liquid sections
- Responsive images, visible focus states, keyboard-aware controls, and reduced
  motion support
- Beginner HTML manual, quick start, FAQ, release notes, and support policy
- Server-side Envato purchase activation for support and update eligibility,
  bound to one permanent Shopify store without storefront DRM

## Requirements and exclusions

Requires a Shopify plan that permits third-party theme upload. Shopify hosting,
domain, apps, store setup, catalogue data, product photography, custom
development, and third-party services are not included. Preview products and
imagery are presentation-only unless explicitly listed in the buyer download.

## Upload checklist

1. Confirm the public preview, documentation, support, privacy, and refund URLs.
2. Confirm the Regular License price and supported-item selection in the form.
3. Upload the 2340×1560 3:2 cover image.
4. Upload at least three preview images from the preview archive, preferably in
   a consistent 1920×1080 presentation set.
5. Upload the buyer ZIP, paste `item-description.html`, and add the tags above.
6. Paste `reviewer-notes.md` into the reviewer message.
7. Confirm every selected browser and attribute has current evidence.
8. Submit only after the legal/name/asset-rights and public-demo gates are
   signed off.
