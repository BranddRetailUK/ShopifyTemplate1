Commerce, clearly framed.

Modeframe is a modern editorial Shopify Online Store 2.0 theme for fashion, lifestyle, beauty, accessories, art, design, home, and other visually led brands. It combines crisp grids, oversized typography, deliberate whitespace, and controlled motion with Shopify-native commerce.

Six global styles in one install

Choose Paper, Ink, Signal, Electric, All light, or All dark from Theme settings. These are coordinated storefront-wide styles inside one Modeframe theme, not separate downloads.

Product-first commerce

  • Product images, Shopify-hosted video, external video, and 3D media
  • Variants, buttons, dropdowns, native color and image swatches
  • Price, compare-at price, unit price, SKU, inventory, quantity rules, and pickup
  • One-time purchase and app-created selling plans
  • Accelerated checkout, Shop Pay instalment messaging, gift-card recipients, and recommendations where Shopify enables them
  • Predictive search, filtering, sorting, pagination, quick add, and drawer or page cart

Editorial section system

Build campaigns and product stories with Hero, Slideshow, Scroll Bridge, Motion Accents, Scrolling text, Rich text, Feature grid, Media with text, Featured collection, Featured product, Collection list, Testimonials, Logo list, Featured blog, Video, Collapsible content, Newsletter, Call to action, and Custom Liquid.

Responsive and considerate

Modeframe uses responsive Shopify CDN images, practical touch targets, visible focus treatment, keyboard-aware dialogs, semantic native forms, and reduced-motion support. Accessibility depends on merchant content, app output, and configuration, so no certification is claimed.

Included in the download

  • Installable Modeframe-1.0.0-theme.zip
  • Beginner HTML merchant manual and Markdown guide
  • Quick-start guide, FAQ, release notes, asset credits, and support policy
  • Envato license notice and purchase-activation instructions

Support and updates

This is a supported ThemeForest item. Six months of item support is included under Envato's Item Support Policy. Purchase activation verifies support and update eligibility for one permanent Shopify store; it never remotely enables or disables the storefront.

Requirements and exclusions

A Shopify plan that permits third-party theme upload is required. Shopify, hosting, domain, apps, catalogue setup, photography, demo content, custom development, and third-party services are not included unless expressly stated. Selling plans require a compatible purchase-options app. Preview imagery and products are presentation-only and are not included in the buyer ZIP.
