MODEFRAME 1.0.0 — QUICK START

1. Unzip the complete ThemeForest download.
2. In Shopify admin, go to Online Store > Themes.
3. Choose Add theme > Upload zip file.
4. Upload Theme/Modeframe-1.0.0-theme.zip — not the complete ThemeForest ZIP.
5. Leave the theme unpublished and choose Customize.
6. Open ACTIVATE-LICENSE.txt and bind the Envato purchase code to the
   store's permanent myshopify.com domain.
7. Add logo, menus, products, collections, pages, imagery, policies, social
   links, market settings, and one of the six Global styles.
8. Preview desktop/mobile and test navigation, search, variants, add to cart,
   cart updates, forms, account entry, and checkout handoff.
9. Duplicate the current live theme before publishing Modeframe.
