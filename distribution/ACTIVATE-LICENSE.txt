One ThemeForest purchase activates support and update eligibility for one
Shopify store, including unpublished Modeframe copies on that same store. The
Envato license shown at purchase controls use of the item.

Activation does not remotely enable or disable the storefront. Never paste a
purchase code into theme settings, Custom Liquid, JavaScript, screenshots, or a
public support message.
