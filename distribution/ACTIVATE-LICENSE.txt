MODEFRAME LICENSE ACTIVATION

Activation URL:
https://modeframe-licensing-production.up.railway.app

After installing Modeframe, enter:

1. The purchase code supplied by the marketplace.
2. The store's permanent your-store.myshopify.com domain.

One purchase activates support and update eligibility for one Shopify store,
including unpublished Modeframe copies on that same store. A second store needs
a separate license unless the marketplace terms explicitly say otherwise.

Activation does not remotely enable or disable the storefront. Never paste a
purchase code into theme settings, Custom Liquid, JavaScript, screenshots, or a
public support message.

If a licensed store must move to a different permanent Shopify domain, contact:
https://brandd.co.uk/contact
