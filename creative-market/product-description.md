# Modeframe Modern Editorial Shopify Theme

Modeframe is a Shopify Online Store 2.0 theme built for fashion, lifestyle,
beauty, accessories, art, design, home, and other visually led stores. Oversized
editorial typography, deliberate whitespace, graphic accent rails, flexible
merchandising, and controlled motion help products feel considered without
replacing Shopify's native commerce tools.

## Six storefront styles

- Paper — warm editorial neutral
- Ink — dark and high contrast
- Signal — vivid acid accent
- Electric — saturated violet accent
- All light — clean neutral storefront
- All dark — neutral dark storefront

All six styles are included in one installable theme and can be selected from
Shopify's Theme Editor.

## Product and commerce features

- Shopify Online Store 2.0 JSON templates and app-block surfaces
- Responsive product images, Shopify-hosted video, external video, and 3D media
- Product variants, native swatches, price states, SKU, inventory, unit pricing,
  quantity rules, pickup availability, and recommendations
- One-time purchases and app-created selling plans
- Accelerated-checkout handoff and gift-card recipient fields
- Predictive search, native filters, sorting, pagination, and quick add
- Drawer or page cart with notes, discounts, properties, and selling plans
- Localization controls, customer accounts, nested navigation, social links,
  payment icons, and Follow on Shop

## Flexible editorial sections

Build product stories with Hero, Slideshow, Scroll Bridge, Motion Accents,
Scrolling text, Rich text, Feature grid, Media with text, Featured collection,
Featured product, Collection list, Testimonials, Logo list, Featured blog,
Video, Collapsible content, Newsletter, Call to action, and Custom Liquid.

## Responsive and considered

Modeframe includes responsive Shopify CDN images, visible keyboard focus,
keyboard-aware dialogs, practical touch targets, and reduced-motion support.
Compatibility has been checked in current Chrome, Firefox, and Safari browser
engines on desktop and mobile-sized viewports.

## Requirements

Modeframe requires a Shopify plan that permits third-party theme uploads.
Selling plans require a compatible Shopify purchase-options app. Availability
of subscriptions, pickup, markets, instalments, accelerated checkout, and other
Shopify services depends on the merchant's plan, eligibility, apps, and store
configuration.

Shopify, hosting, domains, apps, store configuration, catalogue data, product
photography, demo products, custom development, and third-party services are
not included. Preview imagery and products are presentation material unless a
file is expressly listed in the download.
