## License pricing

| License | Price |
|---|---:|
| Personal | USD 79 |
| Commercial | USD 119 |
| Extended Commercial | USD 299 |

The Personal licence is suitable only for a non-commercial implementation.
A revenue-generating merchant storefront is commercial use, so the buyer must
choose the Creative Market licence appropriate to their use. Creative Market's
licence shown at checkout controls the purchase; do not upload a separate EULA.
