## Short description

Modeframe gives fashion, lifestyle, beauty, and creative brands an expressive
storefront without sacrificing day-to-day merchandising. Build product-first
pages with six global visual modes, responsive editorial sections, flexible
navigation, filters, recommendations, selling plans, and accessible motion.

## Full description

Make the product the focal point. Modeframe combines crisp grids, oversized
type, deliberate whitespace, and motion-aware accents in one adaptable Shopify
theme. Paper, Ink, Signal, Electric, All light, and All dark let merchants tune
the whole storefront without installing another preset.

The section library covers campaign landing pages, collection storytelling,
product education, social proof, editorial content, and conversion moments.
Native Shopify data powers variants, swatches, media, subscriptions and other
selling plans, filters, predictive search, localization, customer accounts,
cart, and checkout handoff.
